How it works

1Log each eveningBoring days count just as much.
2Spot suspectsThe days before each episode are compared with other days.
3Later: test on purposeWith enough data, the app suggests changing one thing for a few weeks.
4Get a verdictLikely trigger, probably fine, or still unclear.

When to say yes

Only on days an aphtha hurts. The app mainly cares about the first day of each episode, so painless healing days are fine as a no.

Why sore days are skipped

When it hurts you avoid spicy or crunchy food, which would make those foods look protective. So only clear days are compared.

Honest limits

At about one episode a month, a big trigger can show up within a year. Small effects may never become clear, and they aren’t worth avoiding anyway.

Not a diagnosis. Show a doctor or dentist ulcers that are very large, last over three weeks, or come with fever.
